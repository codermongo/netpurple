About the NetPurple Soundboard

The NetPurple Soundboard is a free online soundboard with 208 meme sounds and sound effects, from viral meme clips to game, TV, and notification sounds. Every sound plays instantly in the browser. No download, no signup, no ads.

FAQ

Is the NetPurple Soundboard free?

Yes. All 208 sounds are free to play, you don't need an account, and NetPurple shows no ads.

How do I use the soundboard?

Click or tap a sound card to play it instantly. Use the search field to filter by name, “Play all” to queue every visible sound one after another, and “Stop” to end playback.

Can I save my favorite sounds?

Yes. With a free NetPurple account you can mark sounds with the heart icon; favorites are pinned to the top of the soundboard on every device you log in with.

Does it work on phones and Chromebooks?

Yes. The soundboard runs in any modern browser on desktop, Chromebook, tablet, and phone. Nothing to install.
