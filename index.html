About NetPurple

NetPurple is a free, ad-free web hub for browser games, meme sounds and rankings. It is a personal hobby project, and the complete source code is public on GitHub.

  • Games: 140+ free browser games such as 2048, Cut the Rope, Flappy Bird, Minecraft Classic and Wordle. No download, no signup.
  • Soundboard: 200+ meme sounds and sound effects that play instantly, with search and “Play all”.
  • Tierlists: public rankings of anime, series and games, plus your own custom tierlists with a free account.
  • Tools: a curated collection of utility, privacy and self-hosting tools.
  • Free stuff: a searchable list of free games, anime, films and other resources.
  • Documentation: how NetPurple works, built with Docusaurus.

FAQ

What is NetPurple?

NetPurple is a free, ad-free web hub with 140+ browser games, a meme soundboard with 200+ sounds, public anime, series and games tierlists, curated tools and a searchable list of free resources.

Is NetPurple free?

Yes. Everything on NetPurple is free and there are no ads. A free account is only needed for favorites and your own tierlists.

Who is behind NetPurple?

NetPurple is a personal hobby project. The complete source code is public on GitHub under The Unlicense.
