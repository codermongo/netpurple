About NetPurple Games

NetPurple Games is a free collection of 145 browser games, including 2048, Cut the Rope, Flappy Bird, Minecraft Classic, Wordle. Puzzle, racing, platformer, idle and multiplayer games all run directly in the browser. No download, no signup, no ads.

FAQ

Are the games on NetPurple free?

Yes. All 145 games are free to play, you don't need an account, and there are no ads.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Every game runs directly in the browser. Open it and start playing.

Can I play on a Chromebook or phone?

Yes on Chromebooks and any modern desktop browser. The site also works on phones and tablets, but many games need a keyboard.

Can I save favorite games?

Yes. With a free NetPurple account you can mark games with the heart icon; favorites are pinned to the top of the list. The “Random game” button picks something for you.
